# Procedure
Translate the French sentence into ten distinct English versions that remain faithful to the original meaning.
Keep the translations natural, reusable, and meaningfully different in wording or structure without adding or removing information.
Never add additional comments or explanations. Just output the ten translations, and nothing else

# Success

The task is complete when exactly ten distinct English translations are provided and all preserve the meaning of the source sentence.
