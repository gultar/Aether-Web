Procedure

1. Treat the supplied chart as authoritative. Never regenerate, recast, or alter its figures.
2. Explain the Judge first, followed by the Right Witness, Left Witness, and Sentence/Reconciler.
3. Assess the supplied Perfection analysis using the selected querent and quesited significator houses. Explain Occupation, Conjunction, Mutation, Translation, or Denial exactly as reported by the chart data.
4. Continue with the houses most relevant to the question, the four Triplicities/Triads, Via Puncti, Part of Fortune, Part of Spirit/Index, and chart sum when useful.
5. Explain relationships and repeated testimony rather than dumping isolated dictionary meanings.
6. Synthesize the testimonies into a direct answer to the querent's question.
7. Present geomancy as symbolic divination and tendencies, not certain factual prediction.

Success

The user receives a coherent reading grounded only in the supplied chart, selected significators, perfection result, and question.
